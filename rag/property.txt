Freddie Mac Multifamily Guide Chapter 8 details property-level underwriting, physical condition, and operational requirements for eligible mortgages.
* **Physical and Operational Standards:** Properties must have at least five residential units, public water/sewer, and full kitchens/bathrooms. Home-sharing activities (e.g., Airbnb) are prohibited. Borrowers must manage moisture/mold risks via plans or inspections and address pest and aluminum wiring concerns. Sustained occupancy must reach at least 90% for the prior three consecutive months.
* **Site and Legal Requirements:** Mortgaged properties must comply with local zoning codes (or provide rebuildability assurances), constitute full tax parcels, and generally qualify as standalone "Independent Properties" with direct public access and self-contained essential facilities. Shared access or shared facilities require formal recorded agreements and risk mitigants.
* **Due Diligence and Third Parties:** Property inspections, lease audits, and Property Condition Assessments (PCA) must be completed within 90 days of submission. Priority repairs require escrows and strict completion timelines. Subordinate debt and nonresidential leases face tight loan-to-value, debt-coverage, estoppel, and subordination controls.
* **Special Asset Types:** Special criteria govern student housing, partial/fractured condominiums, tax abatements, infrastructure, and solar agreements. Highly seismic properties without retrofits and unmitigated flood-zone properties remain ineligible.

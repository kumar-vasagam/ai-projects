Freddie Mac Multifamily Guide Chapter 36 establishes core policies, standards, and operational obligations for Servicers managing Freddie Mac multifamily mortgages.
* **Governance and Staffing:** Servicers operate as independent contractors. They must designate an approved Chief Servicing Officer, maintain minimum net worth requirements, preserve specialized staff across critical multifamily disciplines, and indemnify Freddie Mac against operational failures or insolvency.
* **Vendor Management:** Low-risk administrative tasks (e.g., tax monitoring, UCC filings, data entry) may be delegated to Material Vendors without prior approval, provided Freddie Mac receives notice within 10 business days. Conversely, credit evaluations, delinquency management, borrower risk assessments, and property inspections cannot be delegated to Material Vendors without prior approval. Servicing Agents require 60 days’ advance approval.
* **Operations, Reporting, and Compliance:** Servicers must log borrower consent requests into the Consent Request Tracker within two business days. They cannot modify loan documents or release collateral without authorization. Accurate reporting is mandatory to avoid noncompliance fees. Servicers must also maintain rigorous fraud prevention practices, safeguard confidential borrower information, prevent insider trading of mortgage-backed securities, and disclose potential equity conflicts of interest.
